\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Memory construction and raw retrieval.} Agent memories manage raw history by scoring a memory stream~\citep{park2023generative} or moving context between tiers~\citep{packer2023memgpt}. Other systems extract and link notes~\citep{chhikara2025mem0,xu2025amem}, compress dialogue and images into facts~\citep{liu2026simplemem,liu2026omnisimplemem}, remove duplicate memories~\citep{chen2026telemem}, or organize multimodal memories by entity~\citep{long2025m3agent}. Retrieval can also use confidence weights~\citep{lu2026mma} or modality-specific routes over raw rounds~\citep{kang2026vmem}. Controlled studies find that retrieving raw history matches or exceeds these systems~\citep{bao2026rawrag,zhou2026explicit,peng2026memorytax}; we test this when answers require images.

\paragraph{Navigation and selection.} ReadAgent looks up pages from gist memories~\citep{lee2024readagent}, MemWalker searches a tree of summaries~\citep{chen2023memwalker}, and an agent in LongMemEval-V2 explores stored trajectories~\citep{wu2026lmev2}. LLM rankers order or grade candidates~\citep{sun2023rankgpt,zhuang2024beyond}, and a single selection call can replace fact extraction for text memory~\citep{sharma2026selection}. \vgs{} reads a flat multimodal index to select an evidence set and choose its size. The visibility gate uses the share of a history's words that the index retains to choose a reading path. Selecting complete evidence sets is also the goal of multi-hop retrieval~\citep{yang2018hotpotqa,gutierrez2024hipporag}.

\paragraph{Multimodal memory and reading aids.} MemEye~\citep{guo2026memeye}, Mem-Gallery~\citep{bei2026memgallery}, MemLens~\citep{ren2026memlens}, and related benchmarks~\citep{liu2026worldmemarena,chai2026smmbench,jiang2025memoryqa,nguyen2026personalvisual,nguyen2026camroll,tang2026dmvbench,guo2026seeingsurvey} evaluate memory over conversations and photo collections. Chain-of-Note writes a note for each retrieved document~\citep{yu2023chainofnote}. \vgs{} gives the lightweight reader such notes together with the original rounds on its widened path. A concurrent MemLens study finds that how memory is presented to the reader matters more than perfect retrieval~\citep{jiang2026delivery}.
